DAY 2 – CABOPINO GOLF

Friday 25 September · first tee 11:10 · Men tees · par 71 · 5,409 yards · slope 127 · rating 67.9

Best Ball : Play your own ball, 2 vs 2

Matchplay based on Stableford  |  1 point per match/tee time + bonus = 6 points total

Bonus point : Team with lowest combined strokes on par 3s across the entire team. We add up all strokes taken across the par 3s for all 10 team members. Lowest total wins.

Course tips

The card. Par 71 off the Men tees, 5,409 yards, slope 127 and rating 67.9. Out in 35, back in 36. 3 par 3s (7th, 13th, 15th) and 2 par 5s (12th, 14th).

Where the shots go. Stroke index 1 is the 5th and index 2 the 11th — everyone playing off a handicap gets a shot there first. The 13th is index 18 and is where a shot is worth least, so it is the one to give away if you are choosing your fights.

Where a best ball is won. Two balls means somebody can attack. The sensible split is the higher handicapper going at the stroke holes where his shot is worth most — the 5th and the 11th — while the low man keeps a score on the card. Do not both play safe on the same hole.

The bonus is thin here. There are only 3 par 3s on this course (7th, 13th, 15th), so the par 3 bonus point turns on 30 scores across the whole team. One disaster on a short hole decides it.

Slope, in English. Slope 127 says how much harder this plays for a mid handicapper than for a scratch golfer. That makes it the gentlest of the week, so shots given and received are worth less here than anywhere else we play.

Reading it before you play

Your shots. Count your course handicap, then find that many holes starting at stroke index 1 and working up. Those are the holes where a bogey is still a half.

Their shots. Do the same for the man you are playing. Knowing where he gets one and you do not is worth more than any swing thought.

The bonus point. Team with lowest combined strokes on par 3s across the entire team. We add up all strokes taken across the par 3s for all 10 team members. Lowest total wins.
