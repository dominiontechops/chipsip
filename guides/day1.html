DAY 1 – MARBELLA GOLF & COUNTRY CLUB

Thursday 24 September · first tee 13:50 · Yellow tees · par 72 · 6,071 yards · slope 130 · rating 70.1

Alternate Shot : Brady player 1 tees off, Brady player 2 takes next shot, Brady player 1 holes out, Brady player 2 tees off

Matchplay based on Strokes  |  1 point per match/tee time + bonus = 6 points total

Bonus point : Fewest total putts from a pairing. Track it in the app as you go, and check the other team does it and does it accurately. Partly on the fringe/collar does not count as on green so not a putt, but confirm with the other team.

Course tips

The card. Par 72 off the Yellow tees, 6,071 yards, slope 130 and rating 70.1. Out in 36, back in 36. 4 par 3s (3rd, 9th, 11th, 16th) and 4 par 5s (2nd, 6th, 13th, 17th).

Where the shots go. Stroke index 1 is the 6th and index 2 the 12th — everyone playing off a handicap gets a shot there first. The 17th is index 18 and is where a shot is worth least, so it is the one to give away if you are choosing your fights.

Who tees where. This is the decision that actually wins alternate shot. The even-numbered tees are collectively the harder set here (stroke indexes adding to 72 against 99), and they include index 1, the 6th. Put your straighter driver on the even tees and accept he will not hit the pretty ones.

Irons off the tee. 3 of the 4 par 3 tee shots fall on odd holes (3rd, 9th, 11th). Whoever takes the odd tees is hitting more irons at flags and fewer drivers, which suits the better iron player.

Slope, in English. Slope 130 says how much harder this plays for a mid handicapper than for a scratch golfer. That makes it middling for the week, so shots are worth about what you would expect.

Reading it before you play

Your shots. Count your course handicap, then find that many holes starting at stroke index 1 and working up. Those are the holes where a bogey is still a half.

Their shots. Do the same for the man you are playing. Knowing where he gets one and you do not is worth more than any swing thought.

The bonus point. Fewest total putts from a pairing. Track it in the app as you go, and check the other team does it and does it accurately. Partly on the fringe/collar does not count as on green so not a putt, but confirm with the other team.
