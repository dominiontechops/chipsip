DAY 3 – LA CALA GOLF, ASIA

Saturday 26 September · first tee 11:40 · Yellow tees · par 72 · 5,917 yards · slope 139 · rating 69.1

2 man Scramble : Both players tee off, 2nd shot from the best position, repeat. Minimum 6 tees each, 6 hole outs each

Matchplay based on Strokes  |  1 point per match/tee time + bonus = 6 points total

Bonus point : Pairing with lowest combined strokes on par 5s across the entire team. We add up all strokes taken across the par 5s for each pairing (5 pairings per team). Lowest total wins.

Course tips

The card. Par 72 off the Yellow tees, 5,917 yards, slope 139 and rating 69.1. Out in 36, back in 36. 4 par 3s (3rd, 7th, 11th, 17th) and 4 par 5s (5th, 9th, 10th, 16th).

Where the shots go. Stroke index 1 is the 10th and index 2 the 4th — everyone playing off a handicap gets a shot there first. The 3rd is index 18 and is where a shot is worth least, so it is the one to give away if you are choosing your fights.

Order of play. In a scramble the second man plays knowing what the first has done. Send the wilder hitter first on the tee, so the safe player can respond to a bad one, and reverse it on the greens: the better putter goes last having watched the line.

The bonus lives on the par 5s. 4 par 5s (5th, 9th, 10th, 16th), and the 10th is index 1 as well as a par 5. Two decent drives there and the bonus point is genuinely in play, which is the one place it is worth taking on the hero shot.

Slope, in English. Slope 139 says how much harder this plays for a mid handicapper than for a scratch golfer. That makes it the stiffest test of the week, so shots given and received are worth more here than anywhere else we play.

Reading it before you play

Your shots. Count your course handicap, then find that many holes starting at stroke index 1 and working up. Those are the holes where a bogey is still a half.

Their shots. Do the same for the man you are playing. Knowing where he gets one and you do not is worth more than any swing thought.
